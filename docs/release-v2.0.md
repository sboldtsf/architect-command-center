# v2.0 release kit

## Slack post (paste into the channel)

> :compass: *Architect Command Center v2.0 is out: a full redesign*
>
> It's built around what leadership asked of us for Q4: infuse what's selling, drive architect-led white space, vet real vs fluff pipe, and help close to plan.
>
> *What's new*
> • *Seven tabs:* Today, Pipeline, Deals, Performance, Team, Assets, Settings. Long tabs are split into sections, so each page starts short.
> • *Pipeline:*
>   ◦ Plan and pipe quality first, with every deal tagged Real, Needs vetting or Likely fluff.
>   ◦ A last-quarter check shows how often each tag actually closed.
>   ◦ Q4 priorities come from Slack and Drive, plus attach plays, white space, account plans and where you fit.
> • *One plan number* shared by Pipeline and the Scoreboard, with a scope label on every number.
> • *Prep my 1:1* on Today: a half-page brief you can copy or save as a Slack draft.
> • *Make it yours:* five styles (Futuristic, Simple, Executive, Compact, High contrast) and 16 colour themes, with your team's theme recommended. A one-time setup screen asks for your preferences when you update.
> • *Faster and calmer:* sections load when you open them, animations are off by default, and loading messages say exactly what's happening.
>
> *Get it:* ask Claude to "install the Architect Command Center" (installer skill v2.0), or grab the repo: https://github.com/sboldtsf/architect-command-center
> *Needs:* org62 (required). Slack, Tableau, Tableau Next, Calendar, Drive, Gmail, Lucid and Slides are optional, and features turn off cleanly without them.
> *Already on v1.x?* Re-run the installer; your settings carry over.
>
> Feedback welcome: there's a Share feedback button in the footer. :thread:
