# v2.1 release kit

## Slack post

:compass: *Architect Command Center v2.1 is out*

This one builds on v2.0 and is about trusting the numbers and seeing what moved.

*What's new*
• *What changed since your last visit:* a new card on Today shows stage, close-date, amount and forecast moves on your deals, who made them, and how much your open pipe moved.
• *Check a number, or flag it:* hover a key number and press ⓘ to see how it's calculated and the deals behind it, or ⚑ if it looks wrong. The report is filled in for you, so you just paste it into the Feedback form.
• *Sharper pipe vetting:* deals whose close date keeps getting pushed now score lower and carry a "pushed" badge.
• *Time with no activity type:* Performance › Hours and activity lists meetings that count toward no hours, with a suggested type and a link to fix each one.
• *Update notice:* from now on, the Command Center tells you when a newer version is out.
• Plus general fixes: faster loading, pop-ups that fit the window, and bug fixes.

*I need your help:* I'm still verifying the pipeline numbers. If anything looks off, press ⚑ on the number and send it through the Feedback form.

*Get it:* download the installer from the *Install and Updates* canvas in #architect-command-center, add it in Claude (Settings > Skills > Add > Upload skill), then in a new Cowork chat ask Claude to "Update the Architect Command Center". New to it? Ask Claude to "install the Architect Command Center" instead. Your settings carry over.

*Feedback:* please submit it in #architect-command-center with the Feedback form workflow button. :thread:

## Version history (for the Version History form, no bullets)

Version: v2.1
Date: Nov 6, 2026

What changed since your last visit: a new card on Today shows stage, close-date, amount and forecast moves on your DC deals, who made them, deals you were added to, and how much your open pipe moved.

Check a number, or flag it: hover a key number and press ⓘ to see how it's calculated and the deals behind it, or ⚑ if it looks wrong. The ⚑ fills in a report for the Feedback form. Card headers show how old their data is.

Sharper pipe vetting: deals whose close date has been pushed two or more times this quarter score lower and carry a "pushed" badge. The last-quarter check uses the same rule.

Time with no activity type: Performance › Hours and activity lists meetings with no activity type, suggests a type, and links to each one in org62 so those hours count.

Update notice: when a newer version is posted on the Install and Updates canvas, a banner tells you how to update.

General fixes: faster loading, pop-ups that fit the window, Tableau Next in the connection checks, an updated tour, and a round of bug fixes.
